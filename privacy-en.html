Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · effective 9 November 2026. The Dutch version prevails if the two differ.

Encore ("we") is an iPhone app for learning Italian through music. This policy explains which data the app uses, why, where it is stored and what your rights are. We collect as little as possible: no ads, no advertising identifier and no tracking.

1. Who is responsible

Encore is made by creatof, the Netherlands, who is the data controller under the GDPR. Privacy contact: hello@creatof.nl.

Anonymous usage and crash reports are not linked to your account: the installation ID is a random number kept only on your iPhone. No songs, words or texts are included. Sharing is on by default; turn it off in the app under Profiel → Privacy en gegevens → Je gebruik → Deel anoniem gebruik.

3. Without an account

On first launch the app creates an anonymous user so that progress on your first songs is saved. It has no email address or name. If you create an account later, your progress moves to it. The empty anonymous user is deleted automatically after 60 days.

4. What we don't do

  • No recordings. When you sing along, your iPhone turns your voice into text to compare it with the line. This happens on your iPhone. If your iPhone can't do that itself, Apple processes the speech through its speech recognition (see Apple's privacy policy). Encore never receives or stores audio.
  • No ads and no tracking. No advertising identifier, nothing shared with ad networks or data brokers. We do not sell your personal information.
  • No location, contacts or photos.
  • Issue reports you make in the app stay on your iPhone.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your iPhone. There is no push service.

5. Other parties

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.) hosts our database and sign-in service in a data centre in the EU. Supabase is our processor under a data processing agreement that includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, as Supabase is a US company. Supabase also sends the email with your sign-in code.
  • YouTube (Google). Videos play in YouTube's official player. Google receives data such as your IP address and device information and may use cookies, as an independent controller: see Google's Privacy Policy and YouTube's Terms of Service.
  • Apple. Encore Plus payments, Sign in with Apple and the App Store are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We receive no payment details; the app only sees whether your subscription is active.

Translations and word meanings are prepared in advance with AI and then checked. No user data is sent to the AI service.

6. Retention

  • Account, profile, progress and word list: as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, everything is erased immediately.
  • Anonymous users without an account: up to 60 days after they were left empty.
  • Anonymous usage and crash reports: up to 24 months.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object. Much of this you can do in the app:

  • Access and export: Privacy en gegevens → Exporteer mijn gegevens (a JSON file).
  • Delete: Privacy en gegevens → Account en back-up → Verwijder mijn account. This erases your account and all data on the server and on your iPhone.
  • Object to anonymous usage: turn off Deel anoniem gebruik.

For anything else, email hello@creatof.nl. We reply within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or your local authority.

US residents (including California): we do not sell or share your personal information for advertising, and you can use the same rights of access and deletion.

8. Age

Encore is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Your session is stored in your iPhone's Keychain. In the database, each user can only read their own data (Row Level Security).

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date, and announce significant changes in the app.
